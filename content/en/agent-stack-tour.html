What one client lets you do

  • Code on every machine. Open a session with OpenFox on any of your machines, follow it live, step in, approve. Long tasks go to Kimi Code, delicate ones to Claude Code, everyday ones stay local and free.
  • Keep the thread. Rooms survive a closed app, a lost signal or a change of screen: start on the laptop, finish on the phone.
  • Create. Images, video shots, 3D objects with ComfyUI on your own cards, ordered by an agent that fills in a tested workflow.
  • Watch over the lab. The guard machine (proxy, fail2ban), errors, backups, which model sits on which card: all in one summary, alerts pushed by ntfy.
  • Decide, in one tap. The local orchestrator reads everything by itself and proposes; nothing acts without your tap on a card (see SwarmFox).

Why it stays simple

The concept is ambitious; the building blocks are not. Three rules keep it that way:

  1. Small pieces, one job each. An API that reads, a controller that keeps conversations, agents that code. Each fits in your head, each can be replaced.
  2. The model proposes, code decides. Verdicts, merges and actions are plain programs or your tap, never a model's mood (see BMAD with small models).
  3. One gateway to every model. Change the model, the card or the vendor; nothing else moves (see the model gateway).
