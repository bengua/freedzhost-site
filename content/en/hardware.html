The question everyone asks first: what do I need? Here is what we measured on our own machine, two 16 GB NVIDIA cards (an RTX 5080 and an RTX 4070 Ti SUPER).

The rule: video memory decides

A model runs fast when it fits entirely in the card's video memory (VRAM). The file size of the model is a good first estimate of what it needs, plus room for the context (the "KV cache") and a margin.

System RAM matters too

"Mixture of experts" models only use a small part of their weights for each word, so their experts can live in system RAM while the rest stays on the card. That trades VRAM for RAM: count 20 GB of free RAM for the 35B above.

What to buy first

  • An NVIDIA card with as much VRAM as you can afford. VRAM matters more than raw speed. 12 GB is a comfortable start, 16 GB opens the 27B class.
  • 32 GB of RAM or more if you want mixture-of-experts models.
  • A fast SSD with room: a few models take 50 to 100 GB.
  • Linux. Everything in this series runs on Linux; it is where the tools are first.

Prices change every month, so we do not quote them. Compare VRAM per euro, not benchmark scores.
