The app: Inari

The swarm has a face. FoxBot, the messenger fox, lives in the web console and in the Inari Android app: it rests when nothing runs, types away when an agent works, and tells you when something is waiting for you.

What it costs

It is not free: you need the hardware. With it, you no longer need a subscription. The articles below say exactly what runs on what, with numbers measured on real cards, not on a spec sheet.

What we do not promise

A local model on a consumer card is not a frontier cloud model. It is slower, it makes more mistakes, and we show those mistakes instead of hiding them. What you get is control: your data stays on your machine and nothing locks you in.

For the most demanding

The same building blocks scale. You can grow a very large swarm of agents across several machines and cards. Or you can go hybrid: keep your cloud subscription for the hardest tasks, and put a local agent in front of it, held by deterministic rules. It handles your private data and the routine work at home, and only sends out what truly needs a frontier model. Your frontier quota lasts longer, and your data stays yours.
