Moonshot AI has just released Kimi K3.1 Pro, the first model of its new K3.1 series. It is not a new base model: it is Kimi K3, post-trained with far more compute, to code better, hold long tool-driven tasks better and invent less. It keeps a one-million-token context, and its weights will be published before 4 November.

In short. Available now on Kimi (web and apps), Kimi Work, Kimi Code and the API. Two variants: kimi-k3.1-pro and kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed (2 to 3 times faster, same announced capability, roughly twice the quota). Checked on our side: both identifiers answer on the API, with a window of 1,048,576 tokens.

A bit of history

Moonshot AI, founded in Beijing in 2023, first became known for an assistant able to read very long documents. The K2 series then made its models popular with developers, especially for code and agents. K3, released this summer, brought an open-weight model to 2.8 trillion parameters (the "3T class" Moonshot refers to). K3.1 Pro arrives about three months later: same base, much longer finishing training. It is a strong trend of 2026: a large share of progress now comes from post-training, not only from size.

1. What is announced

  • Code and long tasks: better results on terminal and long-horizon coding benchmarks.
  • Fewer hallucinations: on their hardest internal test, the rate of false claims drops from 23.4% (K3) to 8.3%.
  • 3D creation and financial research: two highlighted areas, with a "3D Make" feature in the app to preview and print 3D models.
  • Reasoning always on: it cannot be turned off; you set its effort (low, high, max, default max).
  • Open weights before 4 November, without the offensive-security training data, deliberately removed.

3. What it costs

  • API: $2 per million input tokens, $10 output, $0.20 for cached input (prices published through OpenRouter on 24 October 2026), with no tier by context length. On the Moonshot platform, the model unlocks after a first top-up of at least $1.
  • Kimi Code subscription: K3.1 Pro from the Plus plan; the one-million-token context and the Highspeed variant from the Pro plan. In the terminal, pick it with /model.

A trap found in the documentation: in a third-party tool, a mistyped identifier does not return an error, it silently falls back to an older model without reasoning. Copy the exact identifier: k3.1-pro in Kimi Code, kimi-k3.1-pro on the API.
